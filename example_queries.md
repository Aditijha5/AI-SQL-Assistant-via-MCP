# Example Business Intelligence Queries
# Use these in the Streamlit UI or agent.py demo

## Revenue Analysis
- Show top 5 customers by total revenue
- What is our total revenue for the year?
- Which countries generate the most revenue?
- Show average revenue per customer
- Calculate monthly revenue trend

## Product Performance
- Which products sell the most and why?
- Show products ranked by total revenue
- What is the average order value for each product?
- Which products have the highest price points?
- Identify products with low sales volume

## Customer Insights
- Which customers have declining purchases?
- Show customers who haven't ordered in 3 months
- Identify our most loyal customers by order frequency
- Which customers buy the most expensive products?
- Show customer distribution by country

## Order Analysis
- What is the average order size (quantity)?
- Show total number of orders by month
- Which month had the highest sales?
- Calculate average time between orders for repeat customers
- Show seasonal trends in purchasing

## Advanced Analytics
- Identify customers who only buy one type of product
- Show products frequently purchased together
- Calculate customer lifetime value
- Find customers with increasing order sizes over time
- Analyze price sensitivity by customer segment

## Operational Questions
- How many unique customers have we served?
- What is the total number of transactions?
- Show the date range of our order history
- Which day of the week has most orders?
- Calculate average order value across all customers

## Strategic Insights
- Who are our at-risk customers showing churn signals?
- Which product categories drive the most profit?
- Identify growth opportunities in underserved markets
- Show customer acquisition trends over time
- Calculate customer concentration risk (revenue from top 10%)
